The details

Why It Works

Greek yogurt

Adds moisture and protein without needing added oil.

Pea protein

Anthony's unflavored pea protein gives the loaf a substantial protein boost.

Banana applesauce

Trader Joe's Apple Banana Fruit Sauce adds moisture and reinforces the banana flavor.

PBfit

Adds peanut flavor and additional protein while keeping the recipe relatively low in fat.

Step by step

Instructions

Preheat

Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.

Dry Mix

Whisk the flour, Anthony's pea protein, PBfit powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt in a bowl.

Wet Mix

In a larger bowl, vigorously whisk the mashed bananas, Greek yogurt, Apple Banana Fruit Sauce, coconut sugar, liquid egg whites, and vanilla extract until fully combined.

Combine

Fold the dry ingredients into the wet mixture using a spatula just until the flour disappears.

Bake

Pour into the pan. Bake for 45–50 minutes. Because this version is lower in fat and sugar, check it at 40 minutes so it does not dry out.

Before you bake

Recipe Tips

Use very ripe, heavily spotted bananas for the strongest banana flavor.
Fold only until the dry ingredients disappear to avoid overworking the batter.
Start checking the loaf at 40 minutes; the lower fat and sugar content means it can dry out if overbaked.
For the most accurate macro tracking, weigh your slice. This finished loaf weighed 824 g.
